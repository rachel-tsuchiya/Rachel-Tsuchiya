# FX Hedging Capability

## Overview

This capability supports the analysis of foreign exchange risk and hedging strategies for Scenario 27: LNG Marketer in FIN 321.

The company is a U.S.-based LNG marketer expecting to receive EUR 21,500,000 from a German utility in 91 days. The objective is to evaluate alternative strategies for managing exchange-rate risk and protecting the USD value of the receivable.

## Hedging Strategies

The financial model will compare three approaches:

1. **Forward Hedge:** Lock in a future exchange rate to secure USD proceeds.
2. **Money-Market Hedge:** Use borrowing, currency conversion, and investment to establish future USD proceeds.
3. **Currency Put Option:** Protect against EUR depreciation while preserving potential benefits from EUR appreciation.
